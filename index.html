Our Story

Where Every Meal
Tells a Story

Founded in the heart of Bangalore, Aesthetic Restaurant was born from a passion to bring world-class dining to India's garden city. We believe food is more than sustenance — it's an experience that connects people.

Our chefs travel across the globe to curate techniques and flavours, blending them with the richness of Indian culinary heritage. Every plate is crafted with seasonal, locally sourced ingredients.

  • Locally sourced, farm-fresh ingredients
  • Curated wine & cocktail pairings

Meet the Chef

The master behind every flavour

"Cooking is not just my profession — it is my meditation, my language, my art."

Chef Arjun Mehta

Executive Chef · 18 Years Experience

Trained at the Culinary Institute of America and Le Cordon Bleu Paris, Chef Arjun brings a global perspective fused with deep-rooted Indian flavour traditions.

His philosophy: let the ingredient speak. From sourcing truffles from Himachal Pradesh to perfecting Chettinad spice blends, he believes in honouring provenance.

He has cooked for state dignitaries, international celebrities, and families celebrating life's milestones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries

Do you provide home delivery?
Yes, we deliver within a 10 km radius. Orders via our website, Swiggy, or Zomato. Delivery time is typically 35–50 minutes.
Do you allow birthday parties and private events?
Yes! Garden Hall (up to 30 guests) and Grand Ballroom (up to 80 guests). Custom packages with décor, menu, and entertainment available.
Do you cater to dietary restrictions?
Yes — vegan, gluten-free, Jain, and allergen-specific menus. Mention requirements at booking or inform your server.
Is valet parking available?
Yes, complimentary valet for all dine-in guests. Dedicated self-parking lot adjacent to the restaurant also available.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancel up to 4 hours before without charge. Late cancellations for special advance-booked events may incur a nominal fee.
Do you have a dress code?
Smart casual preferred for dinner. No beachwear, sportswear, or flip-flops during dinner hours please.
